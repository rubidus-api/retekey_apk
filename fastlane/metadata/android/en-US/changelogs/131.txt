Hindi joins, on InScript's positions as four letter rows and a signs
row. InScript's own system rides the upward flicks: a matra flicks up
to its independent vowel, a plain consonant to its aspirate. The
candra family shares one key, the sibilants and nasal signs share
theirs, and the rupee sign, nukta, avagraha, om and danda have keys of
their own. Conjuncts are your editor's to draw.
