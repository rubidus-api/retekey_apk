Corrections to the last two releases, from the owner's own use.

The 12-key pads hold digits again, the way they always did; the
calculator characters stay on the Keypad layout, which is a pad rather
than a way of writing Hangul.

The 漢 key is back on both 12-key pads while Hangul is being typed. It
was only ever meant to leave the keypad and cursor-cluster overlays,
where there is no reading in front of the cursor to convert.

A field that takes numbers - a phone number, an amount, a PIN, a date -
now opens on the 12-key Keypad.

The action bar appears as soon as it is switched on, instead of waiting
for the keyboard to be rebuilt for some other reason.

Arrows and Keypad are named in English alone, and the layouts setting no
longer talks about a globe key this keyboard does not have or a Hanja
hold it never had.
