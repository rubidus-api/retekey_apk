The action bar's second set of slots.

Clip opens a list of what was cut and copied through the bar: tap to
paste, star to pin, and Clear to forget the rest. Nothing is recorded
from a password or other sensitive field, twenty unpinned clips are
kept, and it never leaves the app's own storage.

Sym goes to the symbols page from wherever you are, and Memo raises the
notepad.

The bar now rides on the floating keyboard too, where reaching for the
menu page cost the most.
