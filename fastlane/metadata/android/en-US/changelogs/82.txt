A key armed for one keystroke now takes the full accent colour instead
of a pale wash you had to look for, and a pressed key goes further
toward it. A key held down wears the same colours as the popup that
shows each keystroke — accent behind, background-coloured letters — with
its label outlined, so armed and held cannot be read as one.

The space key shows the open box that has always meant one space. Keys
read in English outside the Korean layouts, in words that shrink to fit
the key: Undo, Redo, Copy, Cut, Paste, SelA, Date, Clip, Switch,
Manage, Flt, Theme, Size−/Size+. The layout key says >qw for QWERTY.
