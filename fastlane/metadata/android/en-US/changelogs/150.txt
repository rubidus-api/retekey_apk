The pad page's right-hand modifiers (RAlt, RCt, RSh) came alive: a tap arms for one key, a hold locks, and the state survives switching layouts - so the chord can be finished with an ordinary letter anywhere. RSh is a chording Shift for key events (Shift+arrow selection). The same six modifiers are available as action-bar buttons (add them in bar settings). The 12-key layouts' modifier column flipped to Tab/Alt/Meta/Ctrl top-to-bottom, putting Ctrl nearest the thumb.
