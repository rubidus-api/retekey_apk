Keys read better. A pressed key brightens instead of being washed over,
a key held down (Ctrl, Shift, Tab) inverts so it cannot be missed, and
the small hold-hint now sits inside the key's face.

The space bar is a bar rather than the word "space", the key that walks
the layouts says which one you are on (한글 / ABC / 천지 / 나랏 / DVK),
the menu page reads in words instead of pictographs, and no function
key is longer than three characters.
