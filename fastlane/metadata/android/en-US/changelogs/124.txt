Japanese by romaji: type on QWERTY and hiragana appears as you go -
ka, sha, doubled consonants for the small tsu, nn for n, the hyphen
for the long-vowel bar, x or l for the small kana. Kana-to-kanji
conversion is not in yet. Layout names in settings and the switch
toast now read as cap-English(native), like es-Spanish(Espanol).
