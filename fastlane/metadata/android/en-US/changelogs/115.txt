The settings screens no longer hide their first rows under the title bar
(issue #2, seen on One UI 8.5). Android 15 lays activities out edge to
edge and some ROMs put the content under the status bar and action bar;
each screen now measures where its frame landed against where the bars
reach and pads by the overlap - zero on phones that already fit it -
and does the same at the bottom and the sides in landscape.
