ReteKey

An Android Hangul keyboard with the keys work needs

Esc, Tab, Ctrl chords, F1–F12 and the arrows send real key events, so a terminal over ssh, Termux and an editor's shortcuts behave. Korean composes properly on 2-beolsik, Cheonjiin and Naratgeul, Hanja converts both ways, and 34 language layouts share one grid. It asks for one permission, never touches the network, and still runs on Android 4.0.

  • Android 9+, and a build for Android 4.0+
  • APK about 749 KB
  • One permission: vibration
  • No network, no account
  • MIT open source

What ReteKey does well

The keys a terminal needs

Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, Meta, F1–F12, arrows, Home/End, PgUp/PgDn, Ins and forward delete send real key events, not look-alike characters. A soft Ctrl plus a letter sends a genuine control code, and chords the keyboard has no use for go to the app.

Who it is for

Korean done properly

2-beolsik with every compound final and a backspace that undoes one jamo at a time (닭 → 달 → 다), and the 12-key Cheonjiin and Naratgeul phone modes with drags for the letters a tap would cycle to.

The layouts, key by key

Hanja both ways

Convert what you just typed, or a selection, in a window of its own with the 훈음 gloss beside each character, nine to a page and picked with a number key. It appears on every layout, docked or floating if you prefer.

Hanja conversion

Thirty-four languages on one grid

From QWERTY, Dvorak and Colemak to Thai, Hindi, Arabic, Persian, Hebrew, Georgian and Japanese flick. A language's own letters are a flick the mark's own way, and a still hold is always the digit.

Every language layout

At home on a physical keyboard

Assign your own Korean/English and Hanja keys, keep modifier chords, and type 2-beolsik on a Bluetooth or wired keyboard. A US keyboard types the scripted layouts on their own Windows positions.

Physical keyboards

A layout of your own

Write three rows of keys and what each one holds in a text file, share it into the keyboard or open it as a .rkl file, and it is installed. No build, no account, no waiting for a release.
